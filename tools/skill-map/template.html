How your skills tie together

Every file an agent can load, with the links between them. Click a box to read it and follow its links like a wiki. Built from the repo and gitgud, so rebuild after you change a skill.

1the map

Read left to right: what loads first, what the triggers route to, what sits outside it, and the principles. Hover a box to preview its links (amber goes out, grey comes in). Click it to pin them while you look around; click a lit neighbour to move the pin. Double-click, or "open" in the bar, reads the file. The number on a box counts the files that link to it.
