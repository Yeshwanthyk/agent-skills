# Handoffs

**Read when:** work crosses an agent boundary — delegation, resume, or parallel workers.

- A handoff must stand alone: preserve the objective, current scope, material decisions, evidence, completed dependency artifacts, and unresolved limits or resume point.
- The receiving side continues that objective and treats completed dependency artifacts as inputs rather than silently reopening or discarding them. An explanation, plan, or review remains that deliverable unless implementation was requested.

**Limits:** [delegation](../delegation.md) owns worker scope, isolation, review, and accounting; evidence that must survive the handoff is [evidence-lifecycle](evidence-lifecycle.md).
