# Sharpen

Before starting, answer three questions for yourself:

1. **How would a top expert approach this?** Their raw sources, their real methods, and the outside check they would use to know the result is right.
2. **What is the user really after?** The goal behind the request, not only its wording.
3. **Is the task over- or underspecified?** Drop constraints that do not serve the goal as readily as you add ones that do. Leave choices that belong to the user open for them.

Fold in only what makes the work better, not bigger. Always keep one outside check that the result is right; [prove-it-works](../yesh-router/references/principles/prove-it-works.md) covers how to pick it.

Then do the work. Keep the framing out of the reply, and hand back the result, not a rewritten prompt.
