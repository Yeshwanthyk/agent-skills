# Bug fix

Load and follow the canonical [`debug`](../../debug/SKILL.md) skill. It owns reproduction, first-divergence diagnosis, correction, and proof. Do not duplicate or weaken that procedure here.

After Debug completes, run [Opening a PR](opening-a-pr.md). Prepare ordered proof and review material, but stop at its authorization boundary.

## Completion

Debug's completion criterion is met and Opening a PR has prepared the verified change without crossing its authorization boundary.

**Reply:** what was broken, root cause, correction, failing and passing evidence, and remaining uncertainty.
