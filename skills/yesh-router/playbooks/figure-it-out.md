# Figure it out

Use for substantial bespoke work that no other playbook or skill fits: build the smallest workflow that makes an unfamiliar, cross-cutting task tractable and reviewable. Ordinary work runs directly.

1. **Frame.** Write a checkable outcome, scope and exclusions, known blockers, and a rigor level proportional to risk. Decide what evidence makes the result complete, and whether a material product, authority, or irreversible choice needs a human checkpoint. Routine reversible work continues without approval.
2. **Units.** Break the task into meaningful units in dependency order, riskiest unknown first. When the units are a sweep, migration, or stack of similar edits, read [sequence-verifiable-units](../references/principles/sequence-verifiable-units.md). For staged delivery or a blocker caused by missing progress metadata, read [forward-implementation-first](../references/principles/forward-implementation-first.md) to choose units and bound replay. Choose a baseline and a check that separates old behavior from new when applicable; when that check or the work itself is non-trivial to perform by hand, read [build-the-lever](../references/principles/build-the-lever.md). Parallelize only independent slices with explicit ownership, and keep shared mutable state under one owner. Reach for [architect](../../architect/SKILL.md) on an unsettled boundary, [swarm](../../swarm/SKILL.md) for one bounded coverage pass or, through its [best-of](../../swarm/SKILL.md#best-of) branch, competing complete candidates; these are options, not a chain.
3. **Record the workflow** in the task's existing checklist or plan. When the run is long, unattended, or needs later review, keep a [decision trail](../references/decision-trail.md). When the run or its payloads could overflow the session's context, read [guard-the-context-window](../references/principles/guard-the-context-window.md) and delegate or checkpoint accordingly.
4. **Run and adapt.** For each unit, state the hypothesis or intended observable result, make the smallest change, inspect the artifact, and run the relevant check before building on it. When choosing that check or judging a delegated result, read [prove-it-works](../references/principles/prove-it-works.md). Mark each unit verified, not verified, or inconclusive; a failure of the observation method is not a product pass. When evidence changes the plan, log the decision and continue from the new constraint. Delegated roles follow the [delegation contract](../references/delegation.md); reconcile their artifacts. Remove temporary probes. Stop when the outcome is proven or a concrete blocker needs a decision.
5. **Close.** Check the whole result against the original outcome on the real product or artifact, not a proxy. Add a lasting check for a recurring failure only when it serves this task. Record migration, recovery, rollback, or residual uncertainty when relevant. Claim live or publication proof only when it happened. When a decision trail exists, run its audit.

Return the workflow, scope and rigor, units and ownership, evidence per unit, the whole-result verdict, the decision-trail path when one exists, and remaining limits, concise enough for a cold restart.

Done when the workflow is recorded, every unit has evidence or an explicit blocker, the whole result has a verdict against the stated outcome, and a later reader can resume without reconstructing hidden context.
